ESTUN — Camera-Assisted Pick-and-Place

Industrial robot control through a HIKRobot vision system and socket communication, with attention to workpiece localization and orientation.

ESTUNHIKRobot / SCMVSPick-and-placeSockets

Project objective

This academic industrial robotics project integrates an ESTUN robotic arm and HIKRobot machine vision to pick cylindrical workpieces and place them in target locations on a structured workbench.

The workbench has six initial positions and six destination positions. Two intermediate bases support the orientation workflow: one accepts the cylinder without orientation constraints, while the other provides a keyed geometry so that the piece fits only when correctly aligned.

  • Configured machine-vision recipes to inspect the work area and obtain target information.
  • Used socket communication to exchange commands and responses between robot and camera.
  • Developed the pick-and-place sequence in simulation and tested it with the real industrial robot.
  • Developed the orientation strategy around two intermediate bases and the final keyed slots; completing and validating the full sequence remains ongoing.
