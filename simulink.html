Modelling & Control Systems

🚀 Current Focus: Advanced Modelling and Control of Electrical Machines

Simulink & MATLAB Expertise

I specialize in the dynamic modelling of electrical machines, developing high-performance control strategies for both DC and AC motors. My work focuses on bridging theoretical control laws with practical numerical validation.

  • Electrical Machine Modelling: Development of dynamic mathematical models for synchronous and asynchronous machines. I analyze system behavior under various load conditions and transients.
  • Control Strategies: Implementation of advanced control techniques, including Scalar Control (V/f) and Field Oriented Control (FOC).
  • System Optimization: Conducted in-depth stability analysis in the frequency domain. I leverage Simulink to optimize PID regulator parameters, ensuring fast response and minimal overshoot during transients.
  • Numerical Validation: Utilizing MATLAB for data processing and Simulink for validating system response to load variations and disturbances.
