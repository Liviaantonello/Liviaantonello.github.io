Electric Tractor — Modelling & Control

MATLAB modelling of an induction-motor traction system and comparative analysis of starting, braking and V/f control strategies.

MATLABInduction motorTraction dynamicsVariable-frequency drive

Engineering problem

For an electrical machine modelling and control course at Universitat Politècnica de València, I built a MATLAB-based traction model of an electric agricultural tractor powered by a three-phase induction machine. The model couples vehicle resistance and inertia with transmission and motor characteristics.

Methods & analysis

01
Load modelling
Accounted for rolling resistance, slope, aerodynamic drag and vehicle inertia.
02
Electrical machine model
Investigated torque–speed behaviour, the four operating quadrants, energy flow and efficiency.
03
Starting methods
Compared direct-on-line, star–delta, autotransformer, resistive/reactive, soft-starter and V/f-controlled starts.
04
Braking and duty cycle
Simulated braking strategies and intermittent operation, focusing on transient speed and current.

Engineering trade-off

The chosen 600 kW induction machine is intentionally oversized relative to the steady-state load because some conventional reduced-voltage starting strategies would otherwise fail to deliver sufficient starting torque. The report discusses that a variable-frequency drive could enable a more suitable design.

This is an academic comparative simulation study, not a validated specification for a production agricultural tractor.
