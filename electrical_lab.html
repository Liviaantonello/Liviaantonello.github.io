Electrical Machines Laboratory

Experimental characterization of transformers and DC, induction and synchronous machines through measurements and parameter identification.

Electrical measurementsParameter identificationEquivalent circuitsSimulink

Laboratory overview

A series of hands-on tests in an electrical machines laboratory. I acquired measurements, derived equivalent-circuit parameters and operating characteristic curves, and compared experimental observations with analytical or Simulink models.

The documents are original academic reports. PDF reading copies are provided so that results, procedures and diagrams can be reviewed directly in the browser.

Machines and experiments

TransformerWinding resistance, transformation ratio, open-circuit and short-circuit tests.
DC machineArmature and excitation resistance, magnetization characteristics, losses and efficiency.
Induction machineNominal-load, blocked-rotor and no-load tests; equivalent-circuit parameter identification.
Synchronous machineOpen-/short-circuit measurements, Potier analysis and operation in parallel with the grid.

How I used the measurements

  1. Assemble and verify the test circuit and the measurement configuration.
  2. Record voltage, current, power and operating quantities under different test conditions.
  3. Derive equivalent parameters or characteristic curves from the data.
  4. Compare the measured response with the analytical or simulated model.
